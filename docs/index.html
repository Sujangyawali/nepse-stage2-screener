Minervini's entry setups among stocks scoring 7+ on the Trend Template. VCP (Volatility Contraction Pattern): a base where each pullback is shallower than the last and volume dries up. Pivot = high of the last contraction, the buy point. Breakout = closed above the pivot (or a new 52-week high after a consolidation) today on ≥1.5× 50-day average volume; low volume breakouts are weaker. Ready = valid VCP within 5% below the pivot. Stop is under the last contraction low, never more than 8% below the pivot. Not investment advice.
